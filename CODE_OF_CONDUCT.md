# Community Conduct

Help people do good work. Be respectful, specific, and willing to revise your view when new evidence appears.

- Discuss ideas and behavior without personal attacks, harassment, or discrimination.
- Respect privacy. Never post another person's messages, files, credentials, or identifying details without their permission.
- Keep feedback actionable. Explain the problem, its impact, and a possible next step.
- Do not spam discussions, intimidate contributors, or retaliate against someone who raises a concern.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation to protect the community. For a conduct concern, follow the contact route in [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md); if no private contact is available, request one without posting sensitive details publicly.

This is the project's own conduct policy. It applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions when enabled, and other project spaces managed by the maintainers.
